# Open Questions, Conjectures & Hypotheses

This central registry tracks **conjectures** and **open hypotheses** across all four research focus areas so speculative ideas remain strictly separated from established and derived results ([`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) §2).

---

## Focus Area Index

- [`foundations/`](foundations/README.md) — Complexity-theoretic advantages, expressivity, generalization bounds, sample complexity.
- [`variational-models/`](variational-models/README.md) — Ansatz design, barren plateaus, trainability, optimization landscapes.
- [`learning-primitives/`](learning-primitives/README.md) — Quantum kernels, QNNs, tensor network connections, quantum-inspired classical algorithms.
- [`frameworks/`](frameworks/README.md) — Cross-cutting formal proofs, asymptotic scaling analyses, information-theoretic limits.

---

## Registry Rules

1. Every `[CONJECTURE]` or `[OPEN HYPOTHESIS]` introduced in a `research/` note must be added to the table below with a unique ID (`OQ-NNN`).
2. Never reference an entry in this table as an established premise in downstream proofs without clearly preserving its `[CONJECTURE]` or `[OPEN HYPOTHESIS]` dependency.
3. If an open question becomes blocked by a fundamental mathematical or verification bottleneck, link the corresponding ticket from `escalations/`.
